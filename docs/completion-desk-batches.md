# Completion Desk paperwork

Approvals contains multi-file Labor PDF import, pending labor review, and Completion Desk Batch.

PA imports individually downloaded sales-order PDFs together. Each PDF is reviewed separately, matched by Customer ID, uploaded to private storage, and quantities/rates verified. Unreadable or unmatched fields must be corrected. Original files remain attached. The sample supplied extracts CID 684452, 26 priced lines, total $3,252; blank miscellaneous labor is entered as an extra labor request.

Assigned installers enter actual quantities (zero for unperformed work), reasons for changes, and requested extra labor. Contract lines/rates cannot be changed. They review the agreement and sign. FS/Admin approves individual quantities, denies a line by entering zero, records adjustment notes and chargebacks, and signs using their saved profile signature. PA cannot approve labor. Certificate approval and required Lead Renovation signature must precede labor approval. Prior approved labor remains visible; amounts are authorization records, not accounting payment transactions.

Each approved labor sheet snapshots its visit's certificate and Lead Renovation document. Later visits cannot overwrite the earlier packet. Batch screen defaults to yesterday only (exact work date); older or other-day approved paperwork is added explicitly by customer-name/ID search, shows missing forms, and allows up to 20 visit packets per PDF. A PDF index precedes per-customer certificate, approved labor and applicable Lead Renovation record. Original batch PDF bytes are stored privately and are immutable. Downloading is separate from Mark Sent; marking sent records actor/time and job history. No email is automatically sent. One labor approval can belong to only one batch. Later visits use a new labor sheet and certificate. Approved labor is also downloadable from Digital Documents.

Tables production.completion_batches and production.completion_batch_items initialize on authorized office access under a PostgreSQL advisory lock, with RLS and no anon/authenticated grants. Job labor changes use row locks, version checks and history; batch creation locks selected jobs and stores the PDF and membership atomically. No historical job/payment values are migrated.

Customer certificate questions begin unanswered, including after changing certificate type. All questions require explicit Yes/No responses before acceptance; server schema rejects null answers. Email signing links already used unanswered questions.

Validation: TypeScript, production build, operations integration suite (labor permissions, stale checks, protected rates, required lead sheet, approval, archived visit, duplicate batch exclusion, immutable download, sent tracking), PDF rendering and actual sample import extraction. Production rollout still needs a PA/installer test on real devices; no customer emails or payment processing are performed by these tests.

## Blank labor and batch download usability
Installation, completion and service visits can start a blank digital labor sheet directly from the job file without an uploaded PDF. The assigned installer enters work descriptions, actual quantities and requested rates. No rates or labor amounts are invented for blank sheets. Partial drafts can be saved with unanswered quantities/rates and are separate from signed submissions. Uploaded sheets prefill their protected sales-order descriptions, rates and first-visit quantities; actual quantities remain unanswered on subsequent visits to avoid automatically requesting prior labor again. FS/Admin approval remains mandatory for all labor, including service visits. A service certificate remains payment-free.

The batch screen now has a prominent Create & Download PDF action, Select ready jobs, larger checkboxes, a persistent download link after creation and outlined links for saved batches. Rows blocked by missing approved labor explicitly explain why they cannot be selected and include an Open job / complete labor action. Unapproved labor is never bypassed to enable a download. Existing yesterday-only filtering and explicit manual additions remain unchanged.

### Installer labor window
Every assigned job includes **Complete Labor Sheet**, which opens `/labor/[jobId]` in a separate window. Verified labor imports match by Customer ID (a store prefix such as `073-` is accepted); unmatched or duplicate CIDs stay in review. Selecting files alone does not save them: each import must be verified and saved. Existing labor drafts and totals prefill; jobs without labor data show an empty form and save a draft on the first save/submission. Uploaded originals are also available in Digital Documents. Installer-safe job responses include labor sheets, and original labor PDFs use the same private attachment authorization as other job documents.

### LS-2500 digital labor form
The installer form follows the supplied Tampa Bay LS-2500 (Revised 1-20-2022): customer/crew/contact header; Contract Units, Actual Units, Pay and Total columns; instruction, differences, miscellaneous labor and truck inspection sections; installer questions, agreement and drawn signature. New empty drafts use all 27 printed rows and exact reference rates, with blank contract/actual quantities. The sample customer's quantities are never applied to other jobs. Template rates/descriptions are protected on the server; miscellaneous pay and additional requested labor remain editable and require management review. Imported contract details remain authoritative. Notes, checklist answers and truck inspection selections persist with draft/submission. Approved PDF packets use the same paper layout and safe margins; unusually long notes/additional lines continue on an appendix rather than being clipped.

### Automatic uploads
Selecting prefilled labor PDFs now imports each file immediately by its unique CID, without a separate verification/save step. Original PDFs and parsed contract quantities/rates are retained on the matching job. Blank untouched drafts are prefilled automatically. In-progress or submitted installer work is preserved, with the new contract sheet retained alongside it. Identical CID/description/quantity/rate imports are idempotent. Missing/duplicate CIDs and incomplete extraction produce an individual file message and retry field; other files continue. Labor-specific text extraction joins adjacent PDF glyphs correctly and retries image-based recognition of the customer header and labor table when native extraction is incomplete.
